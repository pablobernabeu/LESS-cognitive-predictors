# `paper_2_plasticity/results` — what the manuscript reads

Every number in Paper 2 comes from a file in this folder. The manuscript reads them
through inline R and transcribes no statistic by hand, so re-rendering is what updates the
reported values.

## Naming

| Pattern | What it is |
|---------|------------|
| `_*.csv` | A machine-written summary table the manuscript reads. The leading underscore marks it as a manuscript-facing artefact and sorts the whole set together. |
| `p2_*.csv`, `p2_*.rds` | Model-level artefacts named after the model that produced them, chiefly the projpred solution paths and summaries. Step 8 and its recovery script write these. |
| `07_aperiodic_features.csv` | The one pipeline-internal table. Step 07 writes it and step 04 reads it, and the manuscript never touches it. It carries the number of the script that writes it precisely because it is not manuscript-facing, so leave the name alone: both scripts address it by that literal string. |
| `*.rds` | Fitted brms objects and their per-model summaries. **Not in version control** (`.gitignore` excludes `results/*.rds`), because they are large and are regenerated by the model scripts. Step 6 reads them to build the pooled CSVs. |
| CODEBOOK.md | Column-level codebook, machine-written by ../../_shared/R/write_codebook.R from the file headers and _shared/R/codebook_descriptions.csv. Regenerate after pulling the CSVs. |

Six files arrive from outside this folder's own pipeline, written into both papers'
`results/` so that the two papers cannot report different cohort numbers. **Paper 1's**
steps 0, 0b and 0d write them: step 0 writes `_sample_flow.csv`, `_participants.csv` and
`_participants_other_languages.csv`, step 0b writes `_participant_matrix.csv` and
`_flow_inconsistencies.csv`, and step 0d writes `_training_gate_flow.csv`.

## What writes what

The pipeline table in [`../README.md`](../README.md#pipeline-scripts-run-in-order) maps
each stage to the files it produces, and is the authority. In outline, step 6 writes the
pooled convergence, posterior-summary, attainment and reliability tables from the fitted
`.rds`, together with `_prepost_ns.csv` (the fitted sample sizes of the pre/post models),
`_prior_specification.csv` (the priors the sampler actually used) and, where the fits
carry a per-fit record, `_pooled_fit_metadata.csv`. Step 8 writes the projpred paths.
Steps 9b and 9c write the two model comparisons, `_aperiodic_loo_compare.csv` and
`_aperiodic_kfold_compare.csv`. Steps 10 to 12 write the descriptives the manuscript
injects and the descriptive figures read; step 10 also writes `_task_specs.csv` (the
observed task structure) and `_predictor_values.csv` (the raw predictor values behind the
distribution figure).

Two files are easy to confuse, because their names differ by one word and their
subjects are opposite ends of the same analysis. `_reliability.csv` comes from step 6
and is the reliability of the model's OUTCOME: how much of the between-participant
variance in the by-participant intercept and learning rate the Part A fits actually
recover. `_predictor_reliability.csv` comes from step 11 and is the split-half internal
consistency of the three baseline PREDICTORS. The manuscript reads the first under
*Reliability of the by-participant estimates* and the second in the predictor-reliability
appendix.

`_provenance.csv` is the exception in kind. It is written at fit time by
`../../_shared/R/04_provenance.R` whenever step 04 runs, and records the versions that run
actually loaded together with the seed. Where any other account of the environment
disagrees with it, it is right. The reported Part A fits predate the mechanism, so the
file present here was rebuilt from the version stamps inside the fitted objects by step
6b (`06b_recover_provenance.R`); its `record_source` row says so, and the manuscript
describes the record accordingly. The next completed step 04 run overwrites it with a
record written at fit time. Where the file is absent the manuscript prints
`*[version pending a pipeline run]*` in its place.

## One comparison to read with care

There are two comparisons between the raw-band and de-confounded Part A reference models,
and they are not interchangeable. `_aperiodic_loo_compare.csv` is the pre-specified
PSIS-LOO on the common sample, and it is retained. It leaves out one trial at a time,
though, while the six predictors separating the two models are constant within a
participant, so it is close to blind to what is being compared.
`_aperiodic_kfold_compare.csv` leaves out whole participants, and that is the comparison
the Discussion rests on. The superseded `09_compare_aperiodic.R` writes
`_aperiodic_loo_compare_naive.csv`, and only under `LES_ALLOW_SUPERSEDED=1`; the
manuscript never reads it.

## Reproducing them

None of these files is produced at render time. Run the pipeline (see
[`../scripts/README.md`](../scripts/README.md) and [`../hpc/README.md`](../hpc/README.md)),
pull the CSVs back to the rendering box, and re-render.
