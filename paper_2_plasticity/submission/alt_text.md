# Alternative text for the figures (draft)

Bilingualism: Language and Cognition invites authors to supply their own alt-text at
submission. One sentence per figure, drafted from the captions and the plotting code;
check each against the rendered figure before uploading.

- **Figure 1. Participant flow.** Flow diagram counting participants from enrolment
  through the six sessions to the samples analysed in Part A and Part B, with the number
  retained at each stage.
- **Figure 2. Observed grammaticality-judgement accuracy across sessions, by grammatical
  property and mini-language.** Line plot of mean judgement accuracy at Sessions 2, 3, 4
  and 6, one panel per grammatical property, with a line per mini-language and faint lines
  for individual participants; no session 5 point is shown because no judgements were
  collected then.
- **Figure 3. Pearson correlations among the baseline predictors.** Colour-coded
  correlation matrix of the cognitive indices, the resting-state band powers and the
  individual alpha frequency, with the coefficient printed in each cell.
- **Figure 4. Part A predictor effects on overall accuracy and learning rate.** Forest
  plot of posterior medians with 95% credible intervals for each predictor's main effect
  and its interaction with session, for the pooled and the gender-agreement models.
- **Figure 5. Pre/post change in the executive-function indices.** Paired plot of each
  executive-function index at Session 1 and Session 5, with a line per participant and
  the group summary, one panel per index.
- **Figure S1. Distributions of the baseline predictors.** Histograms or density plots of
  each baseline predictor over the stored records for that measure.
- **Figure S2. Grand-average resting-state power spectral density.** Line plot of the
  grand-average power spectrum over the eight occipito-parietal electrodes, with the
  frequency bands marked.
